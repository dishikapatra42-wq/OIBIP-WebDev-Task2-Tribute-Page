Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam

The Missile Man of India

Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam was one of India's greatest scientists and the 11th President of India. He inspired millions through his dedication, humility, and vision for a developed India. He played a key role in India's missile and space programs and was loved by students across the country.

Achievements

  • 11th President of India (2002–2007)
  • Known as the Missile Man of India
  • Played a major role in ISRO and DRDO
  • Recipient of Bharat Ratna
  • Author of "Wings of Fire"
